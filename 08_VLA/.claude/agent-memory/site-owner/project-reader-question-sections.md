Part III grows from questions the user asks while reading it. Two established homes:
Q&A items in **§13** (grouped `#qa-s1`, `#qa-s2`, … by the section they refer to, numbered Q7 onward, each
ending with a "Back to the page" line), and, when the answer needs its own tables and callouts, an
**unnumbered aside** placed right after the section that raised it (2026-09-23: "Peek: our AiM P1 design"
after §2 — WCK vs CK vs the column path, and how many PU beats fit one column, landing on D).

**Why:** the user reads the published page as a reader and keeps the answers he liked; facts needed by
later sections belong in the body with a link to the answer, not only in the Q&A.

**How to apply:** when a task arrives phrased as "a reader's question the user liked the answer to", keep
the approved answer's content and ordering, make it additive, and pick §13 vs an aside by how much
apparatus the answer needs. See [[never-renumber-sections]].
