Does the Kitsune 3D-IC Story Survive System Scale?

v3 — the scale-ladder study. The v2 pilot modeled ~1/8 of a large GPU. This study reruns the entire v2 experiment battery at 1×, 2×, 4×, and 8× system size — 16 to 128 SMs, 8 to 64 MiB L2 — to answer one question: is the latency-floor story, the mechanism attribution, and the 3D-vs-planar margin scale-invariant, or an artifact of the small model?  16 experiments × 4 scales ≈1,600 simulation runs 4 platforms pre-registered predictions Verdict: same story, slightly stronger for 3D

Companion pages: v2 — the realistic-model report (the s=1 baseline of everything here) and v1 — the mesh-era study. Every number is simulated or calculated on assumed, source-anchored parameters; no hardware calibration.

1 · Executive summary

Same story at every scale — and the physics sharpens it. (1) The queue tax stays a latency floor: PC = 38% at 16 SMs → 43% at 128 SMs; with die distances frozen (ablation A14) it is flat at ~37% — the growth is purely the bigger die's longer wires. (2) Partition crossing overtakes payload transport as the #1 mechanism at 4× scale and reaches 61% of the queue-heavy gap at full scale: on a big die the gap is increasingly pure distance. (3) Design ordering is scale-invariant (B7 ≈ 0.42 recovery everywhere; DSM-class B9 0.68→0.80; 3D 0.96→0.98), and the margins move exactly as distance physics predicts: 3D-over-B7 grows (1.18→1.20×), 3D-over-B9 narrows (1.09→1.06×) because a cluster-local DSM hop, like a vertical hop, does not lengthen with die size. (4) The one reversal: a single global stacked portal (3D-F), tolerable at 16 SMs, is catastrophic at 128 SMs — recovery −0.67, and 5× slower than the plain L2 queue at 4 KiB payloads. Vertical bandwidth must be per-SM/TPC. (5) The 3D requirement gets easier with scale: vertical bandwidth/latency sensitivity shrinks to 4.5%/2.5% spans at s=8. (6) All three pre-registered analytical predictions (PC ↑, B8/B7 ↑, B8/B9 ↓) verified in direction by the DES.

The s=1 rung is not merely similar to v2 — it is bit-identical (the scale machinery is a provable no-op at s=1, checked value-by-value against the v2 battery and byte-by-byte on the BookSim topology files), so every cross-scale difference is attributable to scale alone.

2 · How the model scales (the ladder rules)

One parameter s ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8} derives everything, in all four simulators (custom DES, analytical, SST, BookSim). The rules separate three kinds of quantity:

The √s assumption is bracketed, not asserted. Real cross-generation latency growth mixes wire distance, clocking, and pipeline changes, so every scale experiment also runs ablation A14 — die distances frozen at s=1 values — as the planar-optimistic bracket. Real dies lie between the two curves; the qualitative conclusions hold on both.

Why this is the architectural crux: every mechanism that grows with scale is a distance mechanism, and every planar remedy leaves distance intact (B7) or fixes it only within a cluster's reach (B9). The one design whose queue path length is independent of die size — the vertical hop — is the one whose margin grows.

The scale reversal: a global stacked tier is worse than nothing — again. 3D-F (one vertical access point for the whole chip) was tolerable at 16 SMs (0.31–0.73 recovery in v2). At 128 SMs its recovery is −0.67 at 1 KiB payloads, and at 4 KiB it runs 5× slower than the plain L2 queue (it could not finish its run within the cycle cap). v1 called this design "worse than doing nothing"; v2 softened that on the small model; v3 reinstates it at realistic size. Vertical bandwidth must be provisioned per SM or per TPC — a centralized "3D cache on top" does not work for queue traffic.

8 · Threats to validity & reproducibility

  1. The √s distance growth is an assumption (real latency growth mixes wires, clocks, pipelines) — bracketed by A14; conclusions hold on both brackets, with flat instead of growing magnitudes on the optimistic one.
  2. Weak scaling (per-agent work constant) is one of two reasonable scaling disciplines; strong scaling is untested.
  3. Partitions fixed at 2; 4-partition B200-class dies would push the partition share higher (direction unambiguous, magnitude untested).
  4. One exp213 s=8 3D-F cell hit the cycle cap (37k/64k items) — reported as measured; longer runs would only worsen 3D-F.
  5. All v2 caveats carry over: no hardware calibration, synthetic workloads, B9's stated fairness favors.

Reproducibility: experiments exp201–216 with configs, seeds, and commit hashes; launcher run_scale_battery.sh; plots regenerate via final_plots_s.py; 29-test gate suite including scale-ladder construction checks.
